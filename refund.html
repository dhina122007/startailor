Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Star Tailor provides custom-stitched clothing. Because every order is made to your specific measurements and design, please read this policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Cancellations

  • Orders can be cancelled before stitching begins. Please contact us as soon as possible.
  • Once stitching has started, cancellation is not possible.
  • Cancellation requests must be made by calling or WhatsApp at +91 63817 60498.

2. Refunds

  • If you cancel before stitching begins and an advance payment was made, the advance will be refunded in full within 5–7 business days.
  • No refund is applicable once stitching has begun, as the work is custom-made for you.
  • If the finished garment has a stitching defect caused by us, we will rectify or alter it at no charge.

3. Alterations After Delivery

Minor fitting adjustments (if required due to our stitching error) are done free of charge within 7 days of delivery. Changes to the original agreed design or measurement errors provided by the customer are not covered under this.

4. Online Payments (when applicable)

If you make a payment through our website (when online payments are enabled), refunds for eligible cancellations will be processed through the same payment method within 5–7 business days, subject to the payment gateway's processing time.
