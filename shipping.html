Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Star Tailor primarily serves customers who visit in person for measurements and pickup. Here's what you need to know about how orders are delivered.

1. In-Person Pickup (Primary)

Most orders are collected in person from our tailoring shop. We will notify you via phone or WhatsApp when your order is ready. Please collect your order within 15 days of the completion notification.

2. Delivery / Shipping

For customers who cannot visit in person, we can arrange delivery of completed garments via courier or parcel service. Delivery charges, if applicable, will be discussed and agreed upon before shipping. Star Tailor is not responsible for delays caused by the courier or delivery service.

3. Delivery Timeline

  • Delivery timelines for each order are communicated at the time of order confirmation.
  • Timelines depend on the complexity of the order and the current workload.
  • We will keep you updated throughout the process.

4. Damaged or Lost Orders in Transit

If a garment is damaged or lost during courier delivery, please contact us immediately. We will help resolve the issue with the courier service. Star Tailor is not liable for loss or damage caused by the courier.
