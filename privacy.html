Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Star Tailor, we value your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and contact us, and how we use it.

When you fill a form on our website, we may collect:

We use the information you provide to:

Your requirement and appointment submissions are stored securely on our server. We retain this data only as long as needed to fulfil your order or for a reasonable period thereafter.
