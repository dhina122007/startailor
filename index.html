About Us

Years of Experience.
Personal Attention.

"I started Star Tailor 8 years ago because I believe every woman deserves clothes that fit her properly — not something off a shelf."

At Star Tailor, we stitch women's outfits the way they should be — made to your measurements, your style, and your comfort. Whether it's a designer blouse for a wedding, Aari work on a saree, or a simple everyday kurti, every piece is stitched with the same care and attention.

We understand that every person's body is different, and every outfit has a story. That's why we take time to discuss your requirements before we begin, and we stay in touch through the process so you always know what's happening.

  • 8 years of experience in women's tailoring
  • Custom-fit stitching for all body types
  • Aari work and hand embroidery
  • Designer blouses, Chudi, Kurtis and more
  • Personal attention for every order

Aari Work

Detailed Aari Work,
Made by Hand.

Aari embroidery is slow, careful work — each stitch placed by hand. We do Aari work on sarees, blouses, dupattas and lehengas. You choose the design, we stitch it the way you want.

We work with threads, beads, mirror work and stones depending on your requirement. The timeline and price depend on the design complexity — we'll discuss everything before we start.

The Process

How It Works

Simple, clear and no surprises. Here's what happens from start to finish.

01
Tell Us What You Need

Call, WhatsApp or fill the form. Share your design idea, fabric details and what kind of outfit you're looking for.

02
Discuss the Details

We discuss measurements, design specifics, timeline and pricing. No hidden charges — everything is agreed upfront.

03
Stitching Begins

Once confirmed, we start stitching. We'll keep you updated if there are any questions or changes during the process.

04
Final Fitting

Try it on. If small adjustments are needed, we make them then and there so the fit is exactly right before you take it home.
